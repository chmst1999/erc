Equal Result Credit

Updated October 15, 2026. FBS games only. Teams need three FBS games to be ranked.

Every opponent has one strength number. Beating that team is worth the same thing no matter who did it. A loss is a loss. An FCS game counts as a bye. Margin counts only up to 21 points. There is no preseason ranking and no conference bonus. Colley uses wins and losses only. A Colley rank with a red asterisk differs by more than ten spots, and that week is not settled. Conference is a label, not an input.

How the number is made

Each team has one strength number, called r. It is solved again every Monday from every FBS game played so far. An FCS game is left out, the same as a bye. A team is not ranked until it has three FBS games.

For each game the fit is r for the home team, minus r for the away team, plus about 2 to 3 points if the game is at home. On a neutral field that home term is zero. The margin is the home score minus the away score, and it is cut off at 21 points either way. The ratings are whatever set of numbers makes those games miss by as little as possible. A loss is never entered as a plus. Conference is not in the equation. It is only a label in the table.

A loss does not subtract a fixed number of spots. The sheet is solved again from every game. Alabama’s home loss to Georgia was by three points. Home field is about 2.6 points, so that game says Georgia is roughly 5.6 points better than Alabama. Alabama’s other five FBS games are all wins of more than 21 points, the cap. Those five lines still place Alabama first. Georgia rises because the new game is in Georgia’s favor. The rank is the whole sheet, not the last result.

Colley, the second column, ignores the score and uses wins and losses only. If the two ranks differ by more than ten spots, that team is marked with a red asterisk and the week is not settled.

In the 2024 playoff, ERC had Notre Dame over Georgia and Ohio State over Texas. The committee had those the other way, and the games went with ERC. In the 2025 playoff, ERC had Alabama over Oklahoma, Miami over Texas A&M, and Miami over Ole Miss. The committee had the opposite order in all three, and the games went with ERC. The check is small. It is the games, not a conference adjustment.
