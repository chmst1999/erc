Equal Result Credit

2026, through Week 5. First published ranking of the season. Updated from FBS games only.

ERC gives every opponent one strength number. Beating that team is worth the same thing no matter who did it. A loss is a loss. An FCS game counts as a bye. Margin is capped at 21 points. There is no preseason ranking and no conference bonus. The Colley column ignores margin. If the two ranks differ by more than ten spots, the week is not settled.
